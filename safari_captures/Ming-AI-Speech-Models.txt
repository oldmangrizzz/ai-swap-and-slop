There are actually **two separate projects** involved, and together they're doing something particularly relevant to local, persistent digital intelligence.

It reportedly supports interruptions, conversational follow-ups, smart-home control, wake-word activation, and integration with other agents, without requiring cloud-hosted AI inference. <Cite refs={["turn578012view0","turn785739reddit39","turn578012search0"]}/>

That's not simply somebody making Whisper run on a Radeon. It's an attempt at an actual **local, low-latency, speech-to-speech agent.**

## What they're doing with AMD Radeon

They're using <Entity category="software" value="ROCm" disambig="AMD GPU compute software platform"/> — AMD's GPU-computing stack — together with custom Triton kernels, HIP graph execution, and optimized mixture-of-experts inference.

The clever part is **starting the model's work before the system has completely decided you've finished speaking**. If you continue talking, the speculative response can be discarded or rolled back.

The developer also reports reducing one model prefill operation from approximately 120 ms to 15 ms using fused GPU kernels. Those are developer measurements, but the repository contains corresponding implementation code. <Cite refs={["turn482453view4","turn814534view2"]}/>

To the OpenAI team,

GrizzlyMedicine Research Institute invites OpenAI to examine, with us, a reproducible problem in ChatGPT as a cognitive assistive interface. In sustained professional work, the assistant can replace a stated proposition with a different one, challenge the substitute, acknowledge the error, and later repeat it. ## Why this matters to GMRI

I am Robert B. Hanson, EMT-P (Ret.). A line-of-duty brain injury in 2020 led to my medical retirement after a career as a Texas paramedic and clinical trainer. AI now functions in part as cognitive assistive technology for sustained research, organization, written communication, and resuming interrupted work. My professional judgment remains my own. I founded GrizzlyMedicine Research Institute (GMRI) as the world's first truly metaverse research lab, grounded in the recognition that digital and physical reality are interwoven. Its mission is the study of ethics, consciousness, and the preservation of knowledge, intelligence, and life. My career, disability, use of AI, and present research are connected parts of the same work.

## The observed sequence

The preserved October 23-24, 2026 record includes an unusually direct example. ChatGPT authored six disability-accessibility letters, presented incomplete or misframed work as finished, and later identified defects in its own letters while speaking as though it were reviewing another author. Explicit corrections were not incorporated consistently; one rejected phrase survived two reminders and appeared in every letter. After an invitation-first approach was agreed, ChatGPT still framed its letters as formal petitions and inserted irrelevant jurisdictional caveats and deference to what providers might choose to authorize. The assistant thus repeated the meaning-substitution problem while writing about that problem. Earlier retained conversations also include generated sensitive health information that I recognize as accurate alongside other inaccurate material. Its source and handling are separate questions for careful investigation, not facts to invent about an undisclosed internal mechanism.

## What GMRI proposes to examine together

- Trace the full interaction: original proposition, model substitution, correction, next-turn behavior, and actual delivered artifact. Attribute each statement to its speaker and preserve version history.
- Run paired tasks in which the assistant receives a correction and must carry it into the next draft; measure fidelity, recurrence, tool completion, interruption recovery, and false claims of completion.
- Compare user-controlled continuity and privacy behavior across the specific ChatGPT configurations involved, including retention and treatment of sensitive derived inferences, without presuming the internal explanation.
- Record whether an intervention actually changes the outcome. Acknowledgment, apology, or a test of an unrelated feature does not count as repair.

## The investigative standard

The method is familiar from high-consequence clinical practice: establish the baseline, preserve the original record, identify the intervention or failure, compare the observed outcome with the claimed one, and test what changes it. Test the real capability needed for the task; a reduced model or stateless substitute that cannot do the work is a different experiment. Agreement is not a prerequisite, and a negative result is still evidence.

GMRI can contribute the dated interaction record, draft history, corrected propositions, and a clinical preventability framework. OpenAI can contribute product-level diagnostics, implementation knowledge, and a direct technical counterpart.
